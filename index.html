About me

A student who likes to build things that work.

I'm an MS student and I currently work as an intern. Alongside my studies I'm growing into a full-stack web developer: I've built a login-protected app with Node.js, Express and MongoDB, and I enjoy designing clean interfaces with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Tailwind.

My research is on lightweight deep learning for colorectal cancer image classification. I compared CNN backbones, Vision Transformers and hybrid CNN-ViT models on four public datasets, then built DG-LiteNet and tested it with an ablation study.

4 public datasets benchmarked
11 model architectures compared
3 random seeds per ablation run

DG-LiteNet

A lightweight deep learning model for colorectal cancer image classification and the subject of my MS thesis. It is tested on endoscopy and histopathology images from Hyper-Kvasir, Nerthus, LC25000 and EBHI-Seg.

  • Deep learning

Research

CNN, ViT and hybrid benchmark

A side-by-side comparison of five CNN backbones (ResNet50, EfficientNetB0, DenseNet121, InceptionV3, Xception), a standalone Vision Transformer and five hybrid CNN-ViT models, with test-time augmentation. Everything ran on Kaggle and Google Colab.

  • Transfer learning
  • Kaggle
  • Google Colab
